## Featured project: Study Time and Academic Performance
**Research question:** Does increased monthly study time correspond to higher average academic performance?

Scores rise from 68 to 94 as study time rises from 12 to 35 hours. The visualizations show a strong positive association, although the small illustrative dataset cannot establish causation.
